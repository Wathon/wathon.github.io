About me

With over a decade in tech and the past 2+ years specializing in data engineering, I build scalable data pipelines, automate ETL processes, and deliver robust, end-to-end solutions using Python and modern open-source stacks.

💡 I'm passionate about clean, reliable data and thrive on designing reproducible, efficient workflows that empower data teams and drive smarter decisions.

🔧 Currently leading the architecture of an on-premise data platform built on Kubernetes, Airflow, Spark, DBT, Trino, Apache Iceberg with Nessie, MinIO, and OpenMetadata — purpose-built for transparency, scalability, and version control.

🤝 I thrive in collaborative environments, love tackling complex data challenges, and take pride in crafting systems that are not just powerful, but also elegant, maintainable, and future-ready.

What i'm doing

  • Web development

    Backend development for complex, large-scale systems. I build scalable APIs, manage data flows, and ensure performance and reliability using modern frameworks and best practices.

  • Data Engineering

    Design and build scalable data pipelines, data lakes, and warehouse solutions. I ensure efficient data flow and analytics readiness using cutting-edge tools like Spark, Airflow, and Iceberg.

  • DevOps & Infrastructure

    Professional setup of CI/CD pipelines, container orchestration, and infrastructure automation. I help you streamline your operations with Kubernetes, Docker, GitLab CI, and observability best practices.
