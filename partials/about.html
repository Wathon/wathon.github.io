About me

With 10+ years in tech and the last 2 focused on data engineering, I specialize in building scalable data pipelines, automating ETL workflows, and delivering end-to-end solutions using Python and open-source tools.

💡 I’m passionate about clean, reliable data and love creating reproducible, efficient workflows that support data teams and decision-makers alike.

🔧 Currently architecting an on-prem data platform with Kubernetes, Airflow, Spark, DBT, Iceberg + Nessie, MinIO, and OpenMetadata — all designed for transparency, scalability, and version control.

🤝 I thrive in collaborative teams, love solving complex data challenges, and enjoy making data systems not just powerful, but elegant and maintainable.

What i'm doing

  • Web development

    Engineering the backend of complex and large-scale web systems — from API design to database architecture. I specialize in building high-performance, scalable, and maintainable services using modern frameworks and tools. Whether it’s a business platform, data pipeline, or system integration, I focus on clean design and reliable execution.

  • Data Engineering

    Design and build scalable data pipelines, data lakes, and warehouse solutions. I ensure efficient data flow and analytics readiness using cutting-edge tools like Spark, Airflow, and Iceberg.

  • DevOps & Infrastructure

    Professional setup of CI/CD pipelines, container orchestration, and infrastructure automation. I help you streamline your operations with Kubernetes, Docker, GitLab CI, and observability best practices.
