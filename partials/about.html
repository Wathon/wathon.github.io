About me

I am a Data Engineer with 10+ years of experience across software engineering, backend development, DevOps, and modern data platform engineering.

I specialize in scalable data infrastructure, ETL/ELT pipelines, enterprise reporting systems, lakehouse architecture, and production support for data teams and business users.

My project background spans enterprise data platforms, AWS data pipelines, government-related workflow systems, campaign and OCR platforms, POS/reporting systems, e-commerce/order management, and fuel management systems.

I work closely with analysts, business users, infrastructure teams, QA, UI/UX, mobile teams, junior engineers, and external clients from design through production support.

What i'm doing

  • Backend Infrastructure

    Backend services, REST APIs, admin systems, reporting dashboards, deployment support, troubleshooting, and maintainable production workflows.

  • Data Platform Engineering

    Data lakes, warehouses, ETL/ELT, orchestration, metadata, lineage, data quality, and lakehouse architecture with Spark, Airflow, DBT, Iceberg, Nessie, and Trino.

  • DevOps & Infrastructure

    Kubernetes, Docker, AWS services, CI/CD, Linux, Nginx, monitoring, production troubleshooting, and platform reliability support.
