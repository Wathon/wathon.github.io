About me

I’m a Data Engineer with over 7 years of experience in software development, and for the past 2 years, I’ve been diving deep into building robust data platforms. My expertise lies in designing scalable data pipelines, automating ETL workflows, and delivering end-to-end solutions using Python and cloud-native or on-prem tools.

I’m passionate about clean, reliable data and love crafting reproducible workflows that make life easier for everyone downstream. Whether it's boosting platform performance or making data more insightful, I enjoy the challenge of tuning systems to do more with less.

Currently, I’m focused on architecting an on-premises modern data platform using Kubernetes. My stack includes Apache Airflow, Apache Spark, DBT, Project Nessie, Apache Iceberg, MinIO, and Open Metadata — all orchestrated for scalability, transparency, and version-controlled data operations.

I thrive in cross-functional teams, enjoy solving complex data challenges, and always aim for solutions that are not only powerful but maintainable.

What i'm doing

  • Web development

    High-quality development of modern websites using the latest technologies and frameworks. From landing pages to complex platforms — crafted for performance, responsiveness, and scalability.

  • Data Engineering

    Design and build scalable data pipelines, data lakes, and warehouse solutions. I ensure efficient data flow and analytics readiness using cutting-edge tools like Spark, Airflow, and Iceberg.

  • DevOps & Infrastructure

    Professional setup of CI/CD pipelines, container orchestration, and infrastructure automation. I help you streamline your operations with Kubernetes, Docker, GitLab CI, and observability best practices.
